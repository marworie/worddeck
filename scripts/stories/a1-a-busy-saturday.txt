It is Saturday morning. Lina opens her eyes. The sun is in the window. She looks at the clock. It is eight o'clock.

"Saturday!" she says. "No school today!"

Lina is twelve. She lives in a flat in a big city with her mother, her father and her grandmother. Her grandmother is seventy-five, but she is very active. She walks every day and she cooks every Sunday.

Lina goes to the kitchen. Her grandmother is there. She is drinking tea.

"Good morning, Lina," says her grandmother. "Today is a busy day. Do you remember?"

Lina thinks. "Is it... Dad's birthday?"

"No," her grandmother laughs. "Your father's birthday is in May. Today is your mother's birthday!"

"Oh no!" says Lina. "I do not have a present for her!"

"Do not worry," says her grandmother. "Your mother is at work this morning. She comes home at four o'clock. We have time. We can go to the market and buy food. Then we can cook a special dinner. And we can make a cake."

"A cake! Yes!" says Lina.

Lina's father comes into the kitchen. "Good morning! What is the plan?"

"We are going to the market," says Lina. "For Mum's birthday dinner."

"Good," says her father. "I can clean the flat and buy some flowers."

After breakfast, Lina and her grandmother take a big bag and walk to the market. The market is not far. It is next to the park, near the river.

The market is very busy on Saturday. There are many people and many colours. There are red tomatoes, green peppers, yellow lemons and orange carrots. There is fresh bread, cheese and fish. Lina loves the market. It smells good.

"What does Mum like?" asks her grandmother.

Lina thinks. "She likes fish. And she likes rice with vegetables. And she loves chocolate."

"Okay," says her grandmother. "Fish, rice, vegetables and a chocolate cake. Here is the list. You can read it."

Lina reads the list: "One big fish. One kilo of rice. Four tomatoes. Two peppers. One onion. Six eggs. Butter. Sugar. Flour. Chocolate."

First they go to the fish man. He is old and he is very funny.

"Good morning! What do you want today?" he asks.

"One big fish, please," says Lina.

"For a big family?" he asks.

"For a birthday dinner," says Lina. "My mother's birthday."

"Happy birthday to your mother!" he says. He gives them a big fish. "This is a very good fish. Sixteen euros, please."

Lina's grandmother pays. Then they buy the vegetables, the rice and the eggs. At the end, they go to a small shop for the chocolate.

"Dark chocolate or milk chocolate?" asks the woman in the shop.

"Dark," says Lina. "My mother likes dark chocolate."

The bag is very heavy now. Lina carries it with her grandmother. They walk home slowly through the park. There are children in the park. Some are playing football. Some are riding bikes. A man is running with his dog.

"Can we sit for five minutes?" asks her grandmother. "My legs are tired."

They sit on a bench near the river. The water is blue and the sky is blue too. Some birds are swimming in the river.

"Grandma," says Lina, "what can I give Mum for her birthday? I do not have much money."

Her grandmother thinks. "Your mother does not want an expensive present. She wants something from you. Something from your heart."

"Like what?"

"Maybe you can write her a letter. Or make her a card. Or draw a picture. You draw very well."

Lina smiles. "I can draw a picture of our family. All of us. You, me, Mum and Dad."

"That is a beautiful idea," says her grandmother.

At home, they start to cook. Lina's grandmother cleans the fish. Lina washes the vegetables. Then she cuts the tomatoes and the peppers. She is very careful with the knife.

"Now the cake," says her grandmother. "This is my mother's recipe. It is very old."

Lina breaks the eggs into a big bowl. She adds the sugar and the butter. Her grandmother melts the chocolate. Then Lina adds the flour, slowly. She mixes everything with a big spoon. Her arm is tired, but the cake smells wonderful.

They put the cake in the oven. "Forty minutes," says her grandmother. "Do not open the door."

While the cake is in the oven, Lina goes to her room. She takes paper and her coloured pencils. She draws her family. Her father is tall and he has glasses. Her mother has long black hair and a big smile. Her grandmother has white hair and a green dress. Lina draws herself too, with her favourite blue shoes. Under the picture, she writes: "Happy Birthday, Mum! We love you."

Her father comes home with flowers. They are yellow and white. He puts them on the table in a big glass. The flat is clean and it smells of chocolate and fish.

At four o'clock, the door opens. Lina's mother comes in. She looks tired.

"Hello, everybody. What is this smell? It is wonderful!"

"Happy birthday!" they say together.

Her mother looks at the table. She looks at the flowers, the fish, the rice and the beautiful chocolate cake. Then she looks at Lina's picture. She reads the words. Her eyes are wet.

"This is the best birthday present," she says. She gives Lina a big hug. "Thank you. Thank you, all of you."

They eat dinner together. The fish is very good. The cake is even better. After dinner, they sit on the sofa and look at old photos. Lina's mother puts the picture on the wall in the living room.

"It is a busy Saturday," says Lina. "But it is a very good Saturday."

Her grandmother smiles. "The best days are often the busy days."
