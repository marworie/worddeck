Tom is ten years old. He lives in a small house with his mother, his father and his sister, Anna. Anna is six. The house has a red door and a small garden.

Tom likes animals very much. He likes dogs, cats, birds and fish. But he does not have a pet. Every day he asks his mother, "Can I have a cat, please?" And every day his mother says, "Not now, Tom. Maybe next year."

One morning, Tom opens the door. It is cold and it is raining. He looks at the garden. There is something under the tree. It is small and grey. Tom walks to the tree. It is a cat! It is a very small cat, and it is wet and hungry.

"Hello, little cat," Tom says. "Are you okay?"

The cat looks at Tom. It says, "Meow." It is a very quiet meow.

Tom runs into the house. "Mum! Dad! There is a cat in the garden!"

His mother comes to the door. His father comes too. Anna runs to the door in her pyjamas.

"Oh, it is very small," says his mother. "And it is wet."

"Can we help it?" asks Tom. "Please?"

His father takes an old towel from the bathroom. Tom puts the cat in the towel. The cat is cold. Tom holds it near the warm kitchen. Anna gives the cat some milk in a small blue bowl.

"Not milk," says their father. "Milk is not good for cats. Give it some water and some fish."

Anna gets some water. Their mother opens a small can of fish. The cat eats very fast. Then it drinks the water. Then it sits on the towel and closes its eyes.

"It is sleeping," Anna says. "It is happy now."

"Where is its family?" Tom asks.

"I do not know," says his mother. "Maybe it has a home. Maybe somebody is looking for it."

After breakfast, Tom and his father make a poster. On the poster they write: "FOUND: a small grey cat. Is it your cat? Please call us." They write their phone number under the words. Anna draws a picture of the cat. It is not a very good picture, but it is a nice picture.

Tom and his father walk down the street. They put the poster on a tree, on a wall near the bus stop and in the window of the small shop on the corner. The woman in the shop is very kind.

"A grey cat? No, I do not know it," she says. "But I can ask my friends."

Tom goes to school. At school he cannot think about his lessons. He thinks about the cat. In the English lesson, his teacher asks, "Tom, what is the answer?" Tom does not know. He is thinking about the cat.

At lunch, he tells his friend Sam about the cat.

"A cat in the rain? That is a good story," Sam says. "What is its name?"

"It does not have a name," says Tom. "Maybe it has a name, but I do not know it."

After school, Tom runs home. The cat is in the kitchen. It is not sleeping now. It is playing with Anna. Anna has a piece of string, and the cat runs after it. Tom laughs.

"Did somebody call?" Tom asks his mother.

"No," she says. "Nobody called."

For three days, nobody calls. The cat sleeps in the kitchen. It eats fish and it drinks water. It plays with Tom and Anna every afternoon. In the evening, it sits on Tom's bed. It likes Tom very much.

On the fourth day, the phone rings. Tom's mother answers it. Tom listens. His heart is very fast.

"Yes... a grey cat... yes, it is small... Oh, I see... Okay. Thank you very much. Goodbye."

His mother puts the phone down.

"Is it the cat's family?" Tom asks. He is sad. He does not want the cat to go.

"No," says his mother. "It is a woman from the animal centre. She saw our poster. She says there are many small cats in our town. Many of them do not have a home. She says, if nobody calls in one week, the cat needs a family."

Tom looks at his mother. "A family?" he asks.

His mother smiles. "Your father and I are talking about it. A cat is a lot of work, Tom. You need to give it food every morning and every evening. You need to clean its box. You need to play with it. Every day. Not only on Saturday."

"I can do it!" says Tom. "I can do all of it. Every day!"

"Me too!" says Anna.

One week later, nobody calls. Tom's father comes home with a big bag. In the bag there is a cat bed, a box, cat food and a small red ball.

"The cat is staying," his father says. "It is our cat now."

Tom is very, very happy. Anna jumps up and down.

"Now it needs a name," says their mother.

Anna wants the name "Princess". Tom wants the name "Rocket", because the cat is very fast. Their father wants the name "Mister Grey".

They talk about it for a long time. Then Tom looks at the window. It is raining again.

"I know," Tom says. "We can call her Rain. She came to us in the rain."

Everybody likes the name. The cat says "Meow." It is not a quiet meow now. It is a big, happy meow.

Now Rain is part of the family. Every morning, Tom gives her food before school. Every evening, Anna plays with her. On Sunday, Tom cleans her box. It is not his favourite job, but he does it.

And every night, Rain sleeps on Tom's bed. She is not small now. She is a big grey cat, and she is very happy in her new home.
